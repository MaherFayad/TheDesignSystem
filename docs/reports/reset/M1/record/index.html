Recording pack M1: iOS phone surfaces after order M1

Every clip plays at real speed: each frame is one 60th of a second of the page's own time, drawn at 1x with no zoom. The caption above each phone names the style, the platform and what the finger does; a pink point shows where the finger is down, and a dashed ring where it lifted. Liquid and Neutral on the ios platform, on the iPhone frame, light and dark. The switch is tapped, then held: from pointerdown the thumb lifts into a raised clear lens larger than the track, and it switches on release. The dialog takes the alert form: a 320 wide card with 32 corners and capsule actions. The sheet has 38 top corners and a round close button at the top leading corner, and is dragged down by its top strip. The tab bar and the alert itself have no clip yet: the clip list has no tab bar press and no AlertDialog open (a request to the lead in docs/reports/lanes/reset-M1.md).
