# Sign sheet R0.9: Sample pack for the owner to approve the format

One line per clip. The owner watches each clip at real speed (the index:
`docs/reports/reset/R0.9/record/index.html`) and answers per clip: "signed", or what is wrong.
The lead writes the answer and the date here; the owner does not edit the repo.
Until a clip is signed, its order is "built, waiting on the owner".

## The format (asked once, on this sample pack)

Do these clips show what you need to sign an interaction: real speed at 1x, one phone per clip, the caption with style, platform and gesture above the phone, the pink touch point while the finger is down and the dashed ring where it lifted, the timeline under the phone, and a contact sheet of four moments? Answer "approve the format", or name what to change (for example: both phones side by side in one clip, a bigger caption, dark as well as light in every pack).
